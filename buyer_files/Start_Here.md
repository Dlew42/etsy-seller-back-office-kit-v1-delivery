# Start here: Etsy Seller Back Office Kit

Thank you for downloading the Etsy Seller Back Office Kit. This is an unofficial organization system for Etsy sellers who want one place to track listings, SEO notes, product ideas, profit, SOPs, customer-message patterns, and weekly shop review tasks.

## What you received

You received editable CSV templates and printable Markdown worksheets. No physical product will be shipped. You can use the CSV files in Google Sheets, Excel, Numbers, Airtable, Notion, or another spreadsheet/database tool that accepts CSV imports.

## Included files

- Listing Pipeline
- SEO Keyword Bank
- Product / Offer Library
- Launch Calendar
- Sales + Profit Tracker
- Expense Tracker
- Shop KPI Dashboard
- SOP Library
- Weekly CEO Review
- Customer Message Templates
- Printable Worksheets

## 30-minute setup path

1. Open `Listing_Pipeline.csv` and add your active listings or next product ideas.
2. Open `SEO_Keyword_Bank.csv` and add your best title phrases and tags.
3. Open `Sales_Profit_Tracker.csv` and add your last 7-30 days of shop numbers, if available.
4. Open `SOP_Library.csv` and add the first repeatable process you want out of your head.
5. Open `Weekly_CEO_Review.csv` every week and choose the next three shop actions.

## Recommended weekly rhythm

- Monday: choose three shop priorities and one listing to improve.
- Wednesday: check listing progress, customer issues, and product pipeline.
- Friday: review sales, expenses, profit, and what needs a refresh.
